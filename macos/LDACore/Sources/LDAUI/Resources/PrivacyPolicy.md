# LDA Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026. Version 2026-09-09.

Forme Locale Studio develops Legal Document Anonymizer ("LDA"). This policy covers the native Mac app we distribute. It does not cover independently modified copies, third-party services, or the separate legacy web prototype.

## 1. Local processing, no developer collection

LDA processes document content on your Mac. The app does not send your documents, extracted text, sensitive values, mappings, profiles, or local history to the developer. It has no developer account system, advertising trackers, or automatic usage-analytics or crash-report uploads to us. We do not sell or share app data for targeted advertising, and we do not receive your documents to train models.

Processing on your device still involves information in your documents. Depending on the features you use, LDA reads files and clipboard content you provide, detects information, and produces replacements, filled documents, or restored text. Its local learning features remember your review choices on your Mac, not in a shared training system.

## 2. What stays on your Mac

LDA stores preferences, installed models, and, when relevant to your work, documents, mappings, profiles, vocabulary, learned choices, matter workspaces, and history. Some records include document names and client or matter labels. Separate security and integration audit records use operation details, timestamps, counts, and identifiers rather than document text. These records are local, not developer analytics.

Protected stores use encryption and macOS Keychain features. This does not mean every original, export, temporary file, or clipboard item is encrypted by LDA. Exports and restored files may contain sensitive information. Device access, backups, cloud-synced folders, clipboard syncing, and other software can affect who can access your files.

LDA also saves a local acceptance record containing the date, document versions, copies and fingerprints of the accepted terms and policy, and the app version when available. This record is not sent to us.

## 3. Connections and sharing you choose

Model downloads contact the selected model host and its delivery providers, which may receive your IP address and normal request information, such as the file requested. Downloading a model does not send document content to the host. You can instead import a compatible model file you already have. The host's privacy policy applies to those connections.

If you export, copy, or use a connected AI app or integration, the receiving app can obtain the information released to it. Its own settings may allow transmission to a cloud provider. Even reviewed or pseudonymized text can contain identifying context. Review what is shared and the recipient's terms; LDA's local processing does not extend our policy to other apps.

Opening an outside link also contacts that service. Your operating system and distribution platform may handle diagnostics or download information under their own settings and policies.

## 4. Your choices and deletion

You control the files you provide, whether to download models, and whether to use integrations or share outputs. You can remove stored items through available app controls and delete files you saved. Local information may remain until you delete it or the app's storage is removed. Uninstalling the app may leave application data, Keychain items, exports, backups, or synced copies behind. Deleting one copy does not delete the others, and we cannot remotely retrieve or erase your local documents or recover lost encryption keys.

The app is intended for adult and professional use, not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through it.

## 5. Questions and feedback

For support or questions about this policy, including requests about information you have sent us, contact formelocale@protonmail.com. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond, resolve problems, and improve LDA, and retain it only as reasonably needed for those purposes or to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. Our email provider processes messages to deliver and store them. Do not send client documents, mappings, passwords, or other confidential material; use fictional examples instead.

## 6. Changes

We will date policy updates and make them available in the app. Before introducing developer collection of document content or another materially different use of personal information, we will explain the change and obtain any consent required by law. Acceptance of this policy is not blanket permission for future collection or optional sharing. Mandatory privacy rights under applicable law remain available regardless of the New York choice-of-law provision in the Terms of Service.
