# Connect LDA through MCP

LDA 2.0 window recordings and explanatory cards. All documents are fictional. AI prompts and answers are illustrative. Silent video with English subtitles. Recorded interactions are edited for pacing; timings are not benchmarks.

## 00:00:00.000  Connect your AI workstation

LDA 2.0 connects to supported AI apps through MCP. This walkthrough uses Codex.

## 00:00:05.800  Save the setup script

Keep LDA.app in Applications. Open Settings, choose AI assistants, and select Codex.

Choose Save Setup Script, then open the saved file locally in Terminal.

## 00:00:16.631  Restart and check

Restart your AI app so it loads the connection. Check that LDA appears in its MCP settings.

Ask the assistant to run LDA’s connection check, attest, before starting.

## 00:00:29.000  Give a clear instruction

Choose the LDA skill from the slash-command menu, or use $lda in Codex.

Ask for the work you need. You can choose a Matter locally without naming it in chat.

## 00:00:42.138  Choose files on your Mac

LDA opens a local document picker. Choose the files there instead of attaching originals to chat.

Optional local PII review lets you inspect or add protection before the AI receives text.

## 00:00:55.662  Work with redacted text

The assistant works with the redacted text. Check its answer and any detection-coverage warnings.

Keep every placeholder unchanged so the result can be restored correctly.

## 00:01:06.877  Restore and export locally

For a result based on one document, ask LDA to restore it locally and export it.

Review the saved result on your Mac. Keep each document paired with its own mapping.

## 00:01:20.400  A simple boundary

Keep originals, mapping files and passwords out of chat. Review redacted text before sharing.
